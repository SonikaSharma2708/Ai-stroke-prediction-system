📋 Health Tips

Prevent Stroke With Healthy Habits

Simple lifestyle changes can reduce stroke risk and improve overall health.

🏃

Regular Exercise

Walk daily for at least 30 minutes and maintain healthy body weight.

🍏

Healthy Diet

Eat fruits, vegetables and reduce excess salt and unhealthy fats.

❤️

Monitor Health

Regularly check blood pressure, glucose levels and heart health.

🚭

Avoid Smoking

Stopping smoking greatly reduces stroke chances.

🛌

Good Sleep

Maintain proper sleep schedule and reduce stress.

🥛

Stay Hydrated

Drink enough water daily for healthy blood circulation.
